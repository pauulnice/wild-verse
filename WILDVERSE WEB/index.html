FAQ

Wildlife supports ecosystems and biodiversity. Animals maintain ecological balance, pollinate plants, control pests, and contribute to the survival of our planet.

Support conservation organizations, reduce your carbon footprint, recycle, use sustainable products, and spread awareness about endangered species in your community.

Species become endangered due to habitat loss, climate change, pollution, excessive hunting, and disease. Human activities are the primary cause of most endangerments.

Over 37,000 species are threatened with extinction according to the IUCN Red List. This includes mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, fish, and invertebrates.

Wildlife conservation is the practice of protecting endangered plant and animal species and their habitats. It involves research, protection, restoration, and education efforts.

Many animals can be seen in their natural habitats through responsible wildlife tourism and national parks. Always follow local guidelines and support ethical tourism practices.
